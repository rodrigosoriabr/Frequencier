      How it works

      All together plays every frequency of a set at once. One at a time walks through them in order, the way Rife-style programmes are usually run, and loops until the timer ends.

      Binaural beat plays a slightly higher tone in the right ear. Use headphones.

      Very low (under 20 Hz) and very high (over 16 kHz) tones may be silent on phone or laptop speakers.
